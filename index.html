Each dot is labelled with its identifier: GPS by SVN (space vehicle number), GLONASS by GLONASS number, Galileo by SV ID and BeiDou by PRN. With one constellation shown, colour is the orbital plane; with several, colour is the constellation. Trails fade out over about 11.5 hours — just under one orbit — so each track is roughly a single pass. Starts at your current time and steps forward in 2-minute increments over the span you pick, up to 7 days. Positions come from live CelesTrak element sets; GPS SVN, PRN, plane and block are from the NAVCEN constellation status, and Galileo SV IDs from the GSC constellation information. If the elements cannot be fetched, the page falls back to an illustrative 24-satellite model.
